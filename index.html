    Living outside India? How to buy, step by step

    Wherever you live, you can buy this plot without being in Pune. Indian citizens living abroad (NRIs) and OCI card holders can buy residential plots in India without RBI permission, and you don't need to fly down for every step.

    1. Video walkthrough and documents

      We do a live video call from the plot. You receive scanned copies of the title documents to review with your own lawyer in Pune.

    2. Independent legal check

      Your lawyer runs a title search and checks the records. We cooperate fully and answer every question.

    3. Token and agreement

      A token amount is paid from your NRE or NRO account (or by inward remittance), followed by an agreement to sell.

    4. Registration, in person or by power of attorney

      The sale deed is registered at the Pune sub-registrar office. If you can't travel, a family member in India can sign for you under a registered power of attorney.

    5. Mutation into your name

      After registration, the property records are updated in your name. We help with the paperwork on our side.

    General information, not legal or tax advice. Rules for NRI purchases (FEMA), TDS on the purchase price, stamp duty and registration charges should be confirmed with a chartered accountant or lawyer before paying.

      Questions buyers ask

      Is the price negotiable?

      There is some room for a serious buyer who can close in a reasonable time. Reach out and we'll talk.

      Can I buy it as an OCI card holder, not an Indian passport holder?

      Yes. OCI holders can buy residential and commercial property in India, the same as NRIs. Agricultural land, farmhouses and plantation land are the exceptions, and this is a residential plot.

      Is there any broker involved?

      No. You deal directly with the owner, so there is no brokerage.

      Are there height limits because it's near the airport?

      Plots near airports can need a height clearance for taller buildings. The plot allows 4 to 6 floors. Your architect or lawyer can confirm what applies before you plan construction.

      Can someone in my family visit the plot for me?

      Of course. Parents, siblings or friends in Pune are welcome to visit any day with prior notice.

      How do I pay from outside India?

      Payments go through normal banking channels, either from your NRE or NRO account in India or by an international wire transfer from your bank abroad. Cash is not accepted.
